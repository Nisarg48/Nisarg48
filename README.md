## 🌟 About Me

I'm a **DevOps / Platform Engineer** who also builds fullstack applications. Day to day I work on infrastructure observability, alerting, CI/CD pipelines, and Kubernetes on AWS — and I bring a fullstack developer's perspective to how that infrastructure supports real applications.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Kubernetes (EKS) infrastructure on AWS, CI/CD pipelines, and observability/alerting tooling (Coralogix, Datadog, StatusCake)
- 🛠️ **Core stack:** Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, AWS, Coralogix, Datadog, GitLab, StatusCake, Synthetic Monitoring
- 🌱 **Also building:** fullstack web apps with React, Node.js, and FastAPI
- 👯 **Looking to collaborate on:** DevOps automation, monitoring/alerting tooling, and CI/CD pipeline design
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD pipelines, AWS, or observability & synthetic monitoring setups
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I like closing the loop between "something broke" and "here's the dashboard/alert that would've caught it"
